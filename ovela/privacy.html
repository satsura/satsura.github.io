Ovela Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Ovela is a bedside clock, alarm and sleep sounds app. We built it so that your data stays on your iPhone. This policy explains what that means.

What we don't do

Ovela has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking across apps or websites. We don't sell or share your personal information, and your clock designs, photos, alarms and sounds never leave your iPhone.

One service does receive data, and only around subscriptions — it is described below.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings — clock designs, presets, colors, sound mixes, alarms and night schedule are saved locally on your iPhone and shared only with the Ovela widget on the same device.
  • Photos — when you choose photos as a background, you pick them in the system photo picker. Ovela stores a resized copy inside the app on your device. Ovela never sees the rest of your library and never uploads photos anywhere.
  • Alarm sounds you import — are converted and saved inside the app on your device.

Deleting Ovela removes all of this data from your iPhone.

Alarms, widgets and Live Activities

Alarms are scheduled through Apple's AlarmKit. Widgets and Live Activities are rendered by iOS on your device. None of this data leaves your iPhone because of Ovela.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

To know whether a subscription is active and to see how many people subscribe, we use Adapty, a subscription management service. Adapty receives a device identifier generated by Apple for this app (it does not identify you personally), the country and language of the device, and events about purchases and renewals. It does not receive your name, email, payment details or anything you create inside Ovela. This data is used only to manage subscriptions and to count sales.

Children

Ovela is not directed at children and asks for no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
